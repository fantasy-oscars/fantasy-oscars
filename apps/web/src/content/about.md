This site was born out of boredom with simple games of prediction. We used to wager on the Oscars in the conventional way --- try to guess which nominee would win in each category --- but found that there was substantial overlap in predictions. As we got better at it, we'd converge on the same slate. Didn't make for a very interesting game. The solution? Scarcity! Hence, Fantasy Oscars was born.

This site was put together by individual volunteer contributors. It is through donated time, skills, and expertise that this was possible.

For more information about Fantasy Oscars, check out [how it works](/how-it-works) and [FAQ](/faq).

Interested in contributing?
There are lots of ways to help. If you’re the write-a-check type, donating to a [charity we like](https://donate.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Special:LandingPage&country=US&uselang=en&wmf_medium=wmfSite&wmf_source=10&wmf_campaign=navButton&appeal=WP25&pym_appeal=WP25). If you have ideas or feedback, drop us a line at [feedback@fantasy-oscars.com](mailto:feedback@fantasy-oscars.com).
If you want to help build the app, we’re always happy to accept donated skills and expertise --- especially in:

- Engineering --- building and improving the software
- Design --- improving the look, feel, and usability
- Quality & feedback --- testing, edge cases, and thoughtful critique

Contributions are informal and flexible, and we’ll always help scope work so it’s reasonable for a volunteer. Reach out to us at [contribute@fantasy-oscars.com](mailto:contribute@fantasy-oscars.com).
