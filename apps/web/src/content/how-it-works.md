Fantasy Oscars works like any other fantasy sports type game. Players will assemble a unique roster of players (in our context these are individual nominees) and are rewarded when their roster scores. Best score wins.

## Leagues

Leagues are the groups of people that participate together. A league can (but doesn't have to) live beyond a single event. Play in the same league year after year, or across multiple different awards ceremonies within a season, or both! The league commissioner will set up seasons within the league.

## Ceremonies

Ceremonies are just what they sound like --- awards events, like this year's Oscars. While this site is named for the Academy Awards, it is built to support other film awards ceremonies as well.

## Seasons

Seasons are games built around a ceremony (e.g., 98th Academy Awards) within a league. Seasons are a unique combination of League and Ceremony. This is where rules are set about how that game should be played. The season is also where participants are invited by the commissioner.

## Drafts

Once you've enrolled in a Season for your League, the way you assemble your team is through a Draft. Drafts involve taking turns selecting individual nominees until everyone has a complete roster. Nominees are unique to a category --- that means you can pick Parasite for Best Picture _and_ Parasite for Best International Feature (if someone hasn't already gotten it!).

## Auto-Drafting

If you're unable to attend your league's draft --- or if you're just the planning type --- you can set up auto-drafting to have the system draft on your behalf. We've set up a few basic methods for you.

### Alphabetical

Draft nominees by their name (typically film name, actor name, or song title).

### By Category

Take the first available in the canonical order as determined by the ceremony.

### Wisdom of Crowds

Rely on the past behavior of your peers when drafting for this ceremony. Be warned! You cannot see the aggregated group behavior, so you have to trust that the wisdom of crowds will prevail.

### Custom

Set your own draft order. Order all the nominees in the order you'd like them drafted --- the system will always pick the first available nominee.

### Random

Throw caution to the wind and let the system draft for you in a totally random way.

## Scoring

We've built two different scoring systems.

### Standard

Standard scoring awards one point per nominee that takes home the prize. The team with the most points wins.

### Negative

We also have a scoring system that flips the script --- instead of trying to draft winners, avoid them at all costs! Points are still awarded for each winner, but the player with the fewest points at the end of the night wins.

### Custom

League commissioners can set up bespoke scoring systems where each category has a different weight. You can even mix positive and negative scoring in one season.
